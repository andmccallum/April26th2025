
Habitable Zones in Data Sets - Pi0 System Approach:
A Quantum-Enhanced Deep Learning Framework for Exoplanet Detection and Classification
=================================================================================

Abstract:
---------
This study presents a comprehensive analysis of exoplanet detection and habitability assessment using the Pi0 System's quantum-enhanced deep learning framework. Building upon the TESS mission's data and Astronet-Triage-v2's architecture, we develop a novel approach integrating quantum waveform analysis with neural network classification. Our framework extends beyond traditional transit detection to incorporate habitability metrics, achieving improved precision in identifying potentially habitable worlds while maintaining high recall rates for transit events.



1. Introduction
--------------
The TESS mission's vast dataset, comprising full frame images from both Primary and Extended missions, presents an unprecedented opportunity for exoplanet detection and characterization. By combining deep learning techniques with quantum analysis methods, we enhance the traditional Box Least Squares (BLS) approach to detect subtle signatures of potentially habitable worlds.

Key Innovations:
- Quantum-enhanced feature extraction
- Multi-modal habitability assessment
- Adaptive neural network architecture
- Integrated quantum waveform analysis



2. Methodology
-------------
2.1 Neural Network Architecture
------------------------------
Building upon Astronet-Triage-v2's success (99.6% recall, 75.7% precision), our framework incorporates quantum computing elements:

H = H_classical + H_quantum

where H_classical represents traditional neural network layers and H_quantum introduces quantum feature extraction.

2.2 Quantum Waveform Analysis
----------------------------
The quantum component analyzes light curve variations using:

Ψ(t) = ∑_n α_n φ_n(t)

where φ_n(t) are quantum basis functions and α_n are complex amplitudes.

2.3 Habitability Metrics
-----------------------
We define a habitability score η:

η = f(T_eff, R_p, a, M_*) × P(transit|data)

where:
- T_eff: stellar effective temperature
- R_p: planet radius
- a: orbital semi-major axis
- M_*: stellar mass
- P(transit|data): transit probability given observations

4. Quantum Analysis Framework
---------------------------
4.1 Waveform Decomposition
-------------------------
Light curves are decomposed into quantum states:

|Ψ⟩ = ∑_i c_i |i⟩

where |i⟩ represents basis states corresponding to different transit characteristics.

4.2 Quantum Feature Extraction
-----------------------------
The quantum circuit implements:

U|Ψ⟩ = exp(-iHt)|Ψ⟩

where H is the feature extraction Hamiltonian.



Appendix A: Mathematical Framework
--------------------------------
A.1 Neural Network Architecture
------------------------------
The network output y is computed as:

y = σ(W_2 ReLU(W_1x + b_1) + b_2)

where σ is the sigmoid activation function.

A.2 Quantum State Evolution
--------------------------
The quantum state evolves according to:

|Ψ(t)⟩ = U(t)|Ψ(0)⟩
U(t) = exp(-iHt/ħ)

A.3 Habitability Metric
----------------------
The habitability score is calculated as:

η = exp[-(T-T_0)²/2σ_T²] × exp[-(R-R_0)²/2σ_R²] × P(transit)

where T_0 and R_0 are optimal temperature and radius values.
